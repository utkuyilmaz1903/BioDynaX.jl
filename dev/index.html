BioDynaX.jl

BioDynaX compiles a biological interaction graph into a positivity-preserving UDE and recovers graph-local rational kinetics on unknown edges. This is a research preview, not v1.0: the unique path is gated unknown-edge D(z) recovery (true-monomial recall + hybrid residual versus data on Hill-class edges), not canonical Hill from a trained NN, and not a general CRN or SINDy replacement. Practical k_prod↔D collinearity is reported, not solved. There is no licensed experimental series on the unique-claim protocol.
